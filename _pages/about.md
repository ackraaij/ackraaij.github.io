My name is Sander Kraaij. I work as a postdoctoral researcher and academic lecturer in economics at [Leiden University](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/law/institute-for-tax-law-and-economics/economics/staff). I specialize in labor and personnel economics, using observational data to study inequality and management practices.

Previously, I completed my doctorate at the [University of Cologne](https://www.uni-koeln.de/en/), advised by [Dirk Sliwka](https://dsliwka.github.io/) and [Anna Bindler](https://sites.google.com/site/annabindler/).

## Work in Progress

* **Discrimination in Firing** (with Jan Kabátek, Sacha V. Kapoor, and Dinand Webbink)

* **Firm-Level Corporate Social Responsibility and Employees' Affective Outcomes: A Multilevel Approach** (with Agnieszka Paruzel and Arnoud de Jong)

* **Performance Pay and Ability Sorting: Evidence from Linked Survey and Administrative Data**
